# ITIL 4 definitions (Light Engine)

Public ITIL 4 meanings. One home. Child skills point here.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Service** | A means of enabling value by facilitating outcomes the consumer wants, without them owning the specific costs and risks. On this shop: see `catalogue.md`. |
| **User** | WarPuppy, or a named client (Olivia is C-001). Neo is the desk, not a user. |
| **Incident** | An unplanned interruption to a service, or a reduction in the quality of a service. |
| **Major incident** | An incident with significant impact that needs an immediate coordinated response. Here: P1. Tell WarPuppy now. |
| **Problem** | A cause, or potential cause, of one or more incidents. |
| **Known error** | A problem that has been analyzed but not resolved. Must have a documented workaround or a "none yet". |
| **Workaround** | A solution that reduces or eliminates the impact of an incident or problem when a full resolution is not yet available. |
| **Change** | The addition, modification, or removal of anything that could have a direct or indirect effect on services. |
| **Standard change** | Pre-authorized, low risk, well understood, documented. List lives in `/itil-change`. |
| **Normal change** | Needs assessment and WarPuppy's authorization. |
| **Emergency change** | Must go in as fast as is safe, usually to resolve a P1. WarPuppy still authorizes unless he is unreachable and delay makes it worse — then do it, then tell him. |
| **Service request** | A request from a user that initiates a service action already agreed as a normal part of service delivery. |
| **Event** | Any change of state that has significance for the management of a service or other configuration item. |
| **Configuration item (CI)** | Any component that must be managed to deliver a service. Catalogue rows are the CIs. |
| **SLA** | Agreed target for a service. Shop-floor drafts live in `slas.md`. Do not invent client SLAs. |
| **Value** | The perceived benefits, usefulness, and importance. Ask who is blocked and what they need back. |

Incident restores. Problem explains and prevents. Change makes the alteration safe. Request fulfills something already on the menu. Event notices. Do not mix them in one record.
